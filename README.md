### What I do

I run [Soeteman Software](https://soetemansoftware.nl/), a small company that focuses on [Umbraco CMS](https://umbraco.com/) development. I have been working with Umbraco since V3 or so, and started building packages for it because I needed them myself on client projects. Some of them are still around and used on a lot of Umbraco sites today.

Next to my own products I help clients with their Umbraco projects. Mostly setting things up properly from the start, upgrades from old versions, extending the back-office and lately adding AI where it actually makes sense.

Since this summer I develop my products using only AI. Of course everything is fully end-to-end tested.

### Umbraco MVP

I am an Umbraco MVP, which basically means Umbraco HQ appreciates what I give back to the community. For me that's packages, blog posts and talks at Umbraco events. I still enjoy all of it, and I learn at least as much from the community as I give back.
